# Style Guide Skill

## File Map

- [REFERENCE.md](REFERENCE.md): voice, content-type rules, grammar, and mechanics.
- [EXAMPLES.md](EXAMPLES.md): before/after rewrites and jargon replacements.

## Workflow

1. Identify audience, intended action, and content type. Ask for essential missing facts.
2. Apply the core principles without changing factual meaning.
3. Read REFERENCE.md for the content type. Answer rule questions from that section.
4. Read EXAMPLES.md for rewrites or requested before/after examples.
5. Return polished text and a brief changelog. For examples label Before, After,
   and Changes. Never invent delivery dates, resolved issues, or product promises.

## Core Principles

- Voice: clear, capable, and human; no corporate ceremony or exaggerated claims.
- Tone: calm and direct. Empathetic in support, factual in transactions, useful in marketing.
- Length: short sentences (usually under 20 words) and one idea per paragraph.
- Person: address readers as "you". Use "we" for actions our team owns.

## Instant Rules

- Replace jargon with familiar words. Prefer active voice and natural contractions.
- Lead with the outcome, then the next action. Preserve qualifications and uncertainty.
- Spell out one through nine in prose; use digits for measurements, dates, and steps.
- Use the Oxford comma, sentence-case headings, and correct product capitalization.
- Prefer periods or commas to em dashes. Avoid hype, exclamation marks, and decorative emoji.
- Use inclusive language. Never blame readers or assume identity, ability, or technical knowledge.
