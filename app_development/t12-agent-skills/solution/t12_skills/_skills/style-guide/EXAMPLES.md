# Style Guide Examples

## Email Subject Lines

| Before | After | Rule Applied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IMPORTANT UPDATE REGARDING YOUR REQUEST | Your request is complete | State the outcome, no caps |
| We are pleased to announce new functionality | Try saved reports | Lead with the action |
| Action is required at your earliest convenience | Review your account details | Specific action, no false urgency |
| Notification of scheduled service maintenance | Maintenance on 12 November | Be specific and concise |

## Email Body Rewrites

**Before:** We are pleased to inform you that your request has been processed
successfully by our dedicated support team. Please do not hesitate to contact
us should you require any further assistance.

**After:** We've processed your request. Need anything else? Reply to this email
and we'll help.

**Changes:** Removed ceremony, used active voice and contractions, and made the
next action clear without adding a promise.

## Social Media Rewrites

### LinkedIn

**Before:** We're thrilled to announce our innovative, best-in-class reporting
capabilities that will revolutionize your workflow. #Innovation #Success #Growth
#Technology #Disruption

**After:** A useful report starts with a clear question.

Choose the decision you need to make, then include only the data that supports it.
What question does your next report need to answer?

#Reporting

**Changes:** Replaced unsupported claims with a practical insight, used short
paragraphs, and kept one relevant hashtag and one question. Under 1,200 characters.

### Twitter/X

**Before:** We are delighted to inform our valued users that they may now utilize
our documentation to facilitate the successful configuration of their accounts.

**After:** Setting up your account? Start with the setup guide. It explains each
step and what to check along the way.

**Changes:** Led with the reader's task, replaced jargon, and kept one action.
Under 280 characters; add a real guide link only when available.

## Support Reply Rewrites

**Before:** You have failed to configure the application correctly. As per our
previous correspondence, you must verify that notifications are enabled.

**After:** Missing notifications can make it hard to keep up. Let's check your settings.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Notifications**.
3. Turn on the notifications you need.

You should receive the next relevant notification. If you don't, reply with your
app version and we'll help investigate. Don't send your password.

**Changes:** Acknowledged the impact, removed blame, added numbered actions and
exact UI labels, and offered a specific next step without a resolution deadline.
