Why do GenAI projects fail in production?
Non deterministic behavior and hallucinations
Not transparent answer ("black box" solution)
Issues with LLM security (ex. prompt injection)
Too many expectations (magic LLM) from end users
Vendor misalignment on promise vs. reality
Using GenAI when it's not needed
Trust to 80% quality of PoC
Quality criteria are not defined
Too expensive solution
Special skills are expected from end-users
Unclear goal and undefined ROI
Expectation from performance (< 2 sec)
Issues with integration with existing systems
Garbage in input data and garbage as a result
No input data available in production
Ethical concerns and bias in AI models
